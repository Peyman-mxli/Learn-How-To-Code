# Class 35 — Answer Explanations

[Lesson](./README.md) · [Questions](./QUESTIONS.md)

## Answer 1

**Question:** What is an API?

**Explanation:** An interface through which programs exchange operations or information.

## Answer 2

**Question:** What is a client?

**Explanation:** The application making a service request.

## Answer 3

**Question:** What is a server?

**Explanation:** The service handling and responding to requests.

## Answer 4

**Question:** What is an environment variable?

**Explanation:** A process configuration value, often used with secure secret management.

## Answer 5

**Question:** Where should secret keys live?

**Explanation:** Only trusted server-side environments and secret managers.

## Answer 6

**Question:** Why is frontend validation insufficient?

**Explanation:** Client checks can be bypassed or changed.

## Answer 7

**Question:** What is authorization?

**Explanation:** Deciding whether the identified caller may perform an operation.

## Answer 8

**Question:** Why use a backend for privileged operations?

**Explanation:** To centralize verification, authorization, and confidential credentials.

## Answer 9

**Question:** What should an API error reveal?

**Explanation:** Safe actionable information, not internal secrets or private records.

## Answer 10

**Question:** What is defense in depth?

**Explanation:** Multiple independent controls such as auth, input validation, least privilege and RLS.
