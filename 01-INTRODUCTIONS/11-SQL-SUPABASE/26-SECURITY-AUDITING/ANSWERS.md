# Class 26 — Answers Explained

[Lesson](./README.md) · [Questions](./QUESTIONS.md)

## Answer 1

**Question:** What is least privilege?

**Correct answer:** Grant only the access required for a job.

## Answer 2

**Question:** What is a GRANT?

**Correct answer:** A statement that gives database privileges.

## Answer 3

**Question:** What is REVOKE?

**Correct answer:** A statement that removes specified privileges.

## Answer 4

**Question:** What does information_schema provide?

**Correct answer:** Standardized metadata about database objects and access.

## Answer 5

**Question:** What does grantee mean?

**Correct answer:** The recipient role of a privilege.

## Answer 6

**Question:** What is RLS?

**Correct answer:** Row Level Security, which restricts eligible rows.

## Answer 7

**Question:** Can a privileged role bypass RLS?

**Correct answer:** Yes, certain privileged and BYPASSRLS roles may bypass it.

## Answer 8

**Question:** Why test denial cases?

**Correct answer:** Because security must correctly refuse forbidden actions.

## Answer 9

**Question:** Why avoid logging student data?

**Correct answer:** Logs could disclose private records and create extra retention risk.

## Answer 10

**Question:** What is security auditing?

**Correct answer:** Reviewing effective permissions, changes, access paths and evidence of misuse.
