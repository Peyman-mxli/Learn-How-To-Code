# Class 11 — Answer Key

[Lesson](./README.md) · [Questions](./QUESTIONS.md)

## Answer 1

**Question:** Why do we use JOIN?

**Correct explanation:** To combine data from related tables using an explicit relationship.

## Answer 2

**Question:** What does INNER JOIN return?

**Correct explanation:** Only matching rows based on the ON expression.

## Answer 3

**Question:** What does LEFT JOIN return?

**Correct explanation:** Every left-side row, with matching right-side data or NULL.

## Answer 4

**Question:** What does ON mean?

**Correct explanation:** It introduces the condition connecting rows.

## Answer 5

**Question:** Why use aliases c and t?

**Correct explanation:** To shorten qualified column references and disambiguate identical column names.

## Answer 6

**Question:** What is a foreign key?

**Correct explanation:** A column or set of columns constrained to refer to a candidate key in another table.

## Answer 7

**Question:** Why can a join return multiple rows?

**Correct explanation:** One parent row may match several child rows.

## Answer 8

**Question:** What happens if a LEFT JOIN has no matching row?

**Correct explanation:** The right table's columns appear as NULL.

## Answer 9

**Question:** What does AS teacher_name do?

**Correct explanation:** It changes the displayed column label in the result.

## Answer 10

**Question:** What security applies to joined records?

**Correct explanation:** Appropriate privileges and per-table RLS policies remain necessary.


**Always practice with fictional records in a development database.**
